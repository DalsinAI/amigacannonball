The game: Cannonball, the enhanced OutRun engine, by Chris White and the
Cannonball team, https://github.com/djyt/cannonball.

- The program is under Cannonball's own licence (LICENSE.txt): it may

  build must come with its complete source, and the copyright notice
  must go with it. This is a changed build: its complete source, upstream

  port's own files, are in the Source drawer.
- OutRun is a trademark of the SEGA Corporation. Neither Cannonball nor
  this port is affiliated with SEGA.
- The OutRun ROMs are not here. Cannonball runs the original arcade
  game's data, and those are the player's own: Install-Cannonball copies
  them from the player's drawer or disk into ROMs.
- Res/tilemap.bin, Res/tilepatch.bin and Res/gamecontrollerdb.txt come
  with Cannonball's source (gamecontrollerdb.txt is the SDL game
  controller database, zlib licence).
- SDL 2 is GFOpenUp's (LIBS:GFOpenGPU/SDL2.module), not in this drawer.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/), among them the small stand-in for the Boost calls Cannonball
  makes (amiga/compat/boost, our own code), are under Cannonball's
  licence, like the game. The GlowIcon art is The AmiCradle Team's own.
